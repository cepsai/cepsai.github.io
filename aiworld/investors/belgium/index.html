How to read it

  • Inbound = every equity round raised by a Belgian-headquartered company, with all of its investors placed at their own country of origin.
  • Outbound = every equity round with at least one Belgian investor, placed at the recipient company's country. This includes the Belgian→Belgian part; the rest is Belgian money leaving the country.
  • Dollars are the round total split equally across all investors in the round (Crunchbase gives no per-investor amounts). For a Belgian investor that means their own share; for a Belgian company it is the whole round.
  • Rounds count each round once per source-target pair, so they never double-count a syndicate.
  • Belgian geography comes from each entity's own location record: Belgium → NUTS-1 region (Brussels / Flanders / Wallonia) → NUTS-2 province.
