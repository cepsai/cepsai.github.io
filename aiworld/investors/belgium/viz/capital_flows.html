What this data can and cannot answer

In scope

  • Yes, the two-sided view is constructible — the dataset is built precisely for it: inbound is anchored on Belgian companies, outbound on Belgian investors, over the same round universe.
  • Direction and magnitude of venture flows, by year, round stage, funder bloc and Belgian region.
  • Which institutions drive the outflow, by name.

Out of scope

  • Not actual cheque sizes. Crunchbase publishes no per-investor amounts; every dollar figure here is a 1/n split of the round total.
  • Equity venture only — no debt, grants, public markets, real estate or FDI. This is not the Belgian capital account, only its venture corner.
  • No LP flows. Money Belgian pension funds or families commit into foreign funds is invisible; only direct participation in rounds is captured.
  • No fund domicile. A Belgian-managed fund registered in Luxembourg counts as Luxembourgish — a real distortion for a market next door to a fund hub.
  • HQ-based geography. A company is placed where Crunchbase says it is headquartered, not where it operates.
