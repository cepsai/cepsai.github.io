What the example surfaces

A disagreement the priority rule hides

Our lineages do not agree on what Clingendael is. robert_type says 12, non-governmental think tanks. docx_category and malte_actor_category both read it as a network, which maps onto items 10 or 11. Our own resolved_actor_type is network. We publish 12 because robert_type is the only lineage that codes the partners' 21-item list directly — so the derivation's priority rule settles the case without recording that it was contested.

This is not the ambiguity the register already counts. The 33 actors flagged ambiguous: are cases where a single lineage's label maps onto more than one of the 21 items; Clingendael is not one of them. 07
Recommendation

Where player-level taxonomy output should live

Not the grey-literature overview

That ledger's unit is documents — 375 reports, strategies, portals and indices across 41 countries. Players are a different unit of analysis. Adding actor rows would make the same page answer both “what has been written” and “who acts”, and neither filter would then be trustworthy.

Not all 261 players either

Extending the register page with 29 columns per actor would leave 8 columns identical on every row, 9 columns carrying a guess, and, without a fetch per actor, most of the rest empty. The page would read as a data-quality failure rather than as a taxonomy review.

A review page on four to five contrasting players

Each rendered as this one is: Clingendael (think tank / network ambiguity), NWO (leader tier, explicit science-diplomacy unit), the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (where the country-inherited fields genuinely do belong to the actor), a university (indicator 13 — institution or faculty?), and OPCW (one of the 12 actors the 21-item list has no slot for).

What this fiche cost, and what that implies

Roughly an hour of fetching per actor filled 5 of 29 indicators. At that rate the full NL register is about 261 hours of collection — and 9 indicators would still rest on judgement, because those wait on partner decisions rather than on data. Deciding the value sets and evidence routes first is cheaper than collecting around them.

Open questions for the Advisory Board

Which unit does Size mean — FTE, headcount or turnover? All three are in this fiche and they disagree. What value set does Institutional level take? Is concentrated / dispersed about the actor or the system? Does the 21-item actor list permit an actor to be both a think tank and a network? And do the Lopez & Schunz roles apply to national actors at all, or only to the EU? Each of these turns one of the 9 guesses into a value.
